The four 30-second clips from [Seedance](video-prompts.md) came out with their dialogue, voices, music, and sound already in place. I put them together in **Final Cut Pro**, which I'd practiced with in week 7.

## What I added

- **Character introductions.** Name and title cards for the three main characters (Oliver, Adrian, and Vanessa) and for Celeste, the ex, when each first appears.
- **Transitions.** Fade and flash transitions between clips, to help them flow together as one story.

## What Claude added

- **Captions.**
- **The "ding"** sound effect at the end, the short-drama signature, with a **glow effect**.
