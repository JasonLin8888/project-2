## Week 4 · Choosing the project

*Class on September 30*

I started thinking about what project to do and settled on the idea of an AI short drama after seeing an ad for them on Instagram Reels.

## Week 5 · Plot and inspiration

*Class on October 7*

I thought about the plot and what could be interesting to see. I was working through some ideas with Claude and watching AI dramas and other media (webtoons and Instagram Reels) for inspiration for my AI short's plot and visual look.

This is also when I found common problems that I need to tackle, such as voices sounding weird or text showing up weirdly.

More: [Inspiration board](/inspiration/inspiration-board) · [Early ideas](/brainstorming/early-ideas) · [Finding the plot](/brainstorming/finding-the-plot) · [Preventing common errors](preventing-common-errors.md)

## Week 6 · Character sheets

*Class on October 14*

I chose character sheets as the skill to practice for my project.

1. **General ideas.** I came up with some general ideas of what to make:
   - a cute mascot
   - a hot, cool guy that's protective of the one he cares about
   - a cute, delicate guy who looks like he needs protecting
   - a fierce, confident leader as the female love interest
2. **References.** I found images through Pinterest and Google.
3. **Design.** I had ChatGPT create an image and kept giving it edits until I was settled on the design.
4. **Character sheet.** I created a character sheet of different angles so the AI generation wouldn't mess up the character design.

More: [Characters and voices](characters-and-voices.md) · [Designing Mio](designing-mio.md) · [Places and faces](/inspiration/places-and-faces)

## Week 7 · Editing

*Class on October 21*

I practiced editing videos in **Final Cut Pro** so that I could edit my AI-generated videos together.

## Week 8 · Making it

*Class on October 28*

I gathered my context package, created the videos with the **Seedance 2.5** model on **Higgsfield**, edited them in **Final Cut Pro**, and put it all together on this website.
