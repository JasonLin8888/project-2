## ChatGPT's script

**Oliver Ashford** would fit your MC well: soft and distinctly British, with **“Ollie”** as the childhood nickname his friend still uses. Alternatives: **Theo Whitmore**, **Julian Fairchild**, or **Elliot Hawthorne**.

For Oliver’s appearance, I’d specify an adult in his mid-twenties: 5′10″, around 150 pounds, slender build, fine blond hair, blue eyes, and delicate features. In the opening, make him look pitiful through his behavior: clutching an overstuffed bag, trying to straighten his clothes, and struggling to keep his voice steady.

Make the ex’s villainess quality come from **her composure and deliberate cruelty**: immaculate hair, expensive clothes, sharply arched brows, and a smile that disappears when Oliver stops looking. Her new girlfriend can simply watch from inside the apartment.

Here’s a compact dialogue pass. These are working names for the other characters:

  - **Childhood friend:** Ethan Chen
  - **Guildmaster:** Vanessa Hayes
  - **Ex-girlfriend:** Celeste

Your full sequence is dense for two minutes. This version uses brief exchanges and visual storytelling; let each ending pose settle for about **half a second to one second**.

#### Scene 1: Thrown Out

The apartment door flies open. Celeste throws Oliver’s bag into the hallway. Clothing spills across the floor.

**OLIVER:** “You said she was just a friend.”

Celeste’s new girlfriend appears behind her, wearing Celeste’s robe.

**CELESTE:** “And you said you’d make something of yourself.”

Oliver kneels to gather his clothes.

**OLIVER:** “Can we at least talk?”

**CELESTE:** “We just did.”

**ENDING POSE:** Oliver kneels in the foreground, one shirt clutched in his hand. Celeste stands above him in the doorway, smiling. The door slams.

---

#### Scene 2: The Childhood Friend

Outside, Oliver struggles with his broken bag. A hand catches its slipping strap.

**ETHAN:** “Ollie?”

Oliver looks up.

**OLIVER:** “Please don’t ask.”

Ethan takes the bag.

**ETHAN:** “Come home with me.”

**OLIVER:** “For tonight?”

**ETHAN:** “For as long as you need.”

**ENDING POSE:** Ethan holds the bag over one shoulder and shelters Oliver beneath his umbrella. Oliver looks sideways at him.

---

#### Scene 3: The Next Morning

Quick cuts: two mugs on the counter; Oliver borrowing a shirt; Ethan adjusting Oliver’s crooked collar.

**OLIVER:** “You’re enjoying this.”

**ETHAN:** “You’ve missed a button.”

Oliver looks down. Ethan smiles.

They leave together.

**NARRATOR:** “A core gives a hunter power. Every gate they enter corrupts it.”

Cut to dark veins spreading beneath a hunter’s skin.

**NARRATOR:** “Oliver Ashford can reverse that corruption.”

His healing light clears the veins.

At the office entrance, Ethan taps his Hunter Management badge. Oliver turns toward the guild elevators.

**ETHAN:** “Dinner at seven?”

**OLIVER:** “I’ll try.”

**ETHAN:** “That means you’ll forget.”

**ENDING POSE:** They look back at each other as the elevator doors begin to close.

---

#### Scene 4: The Creature And The System

A rapid memory: Oliver crouches beside a tiny injured creature, cupping healing light around it.

**OLIVER:** “Easy. I’ve got you.”

The creature’s eyes open. For an instant, an enormous magical silhouette rises behind it.

Cut to the present: the little creature perches on Oliver’s shoulder.

**MIO:** “You saved my life. Let me return the favor.”

A translucent system appears:

**CORE CORRUPTION DETECTED.**

**ENDING POSE:** Oliver turns toward the floating interface. Mio hovers beside his cheek, looking unexpectedly pleased with himself.

---

#### Scene 5: The Gate Raid

Vanessa drives her sword through a monster, then looks back toward Oliver.

**VANESSA:** “Ashford. Stay close.”

Oliver heals a wounded teammate. Black corruption retreats beneath his hands.

Something lunges from behind him.

**VANESSA:** “Oliver!”

She tackles him clear. Her blade strikes the ground beside the creature.

They land with Vanessa braced above him. Her free hand supports the back of his head.

**VANESSA:** “Are you hurt?”

**OLIVER:** “I… don’t think so.”

Her eyes move across his face.

**VANESSA:** “Good.”

**ENDING POSE:** Her hair hangs beside his face. Oliver’s hand has caught her sleeve. Their eyes lock; his cheeks redden.

Hard cut to the gate collapsing.

---

#### Scene 6: The Guild Office

Oliver tends a cut on Vanessa’s hand.

**OLIVER:** “You broke formation for me.”

**VANESSA:** “You were in danger.”

**OLIVER:** “I’m your healer.”

She catches his hand before he pulls away.

**VANESSA:** “Is that all you think you are to me?”

**ENDING POSE:** Vanessa sits at the edge of her desk, holding his hand between them. Oliver stands close, caught halfway through an answer.

---

#### Scene 7: Home

Oliver and Ethan arrive at their apartment almost together.

**ETHAN:** “You missed dinner.”

**OLIVER:** “The raid ran late.”

Ethan notices blood on his collar.

**ETHAN:** “Whose blood?”

**OLIVER:** “I’m fine.”

Oliver steps backward as Ethan moves closer. Ethan plants one arm beside his head, cushioning it with his palm before it touches the wall.

**OLIVER:** “Ethan…”

**ETHAN:** “I’ve liked you since we were kids.”

Oliver goes still.

**OLIVER:** “Why didn’t you tell me?”

**ETHAN:** “You kept falling for other people.”

**ENDING POSE:** Ethan waits, close enough to kiss him. Oliver grips the front of Ethan’s coat.

---

#### Scene 8: The Elevator

The next morning. Commuters crowd into the elevator.

Oliver is pushed forward. This time, Ethan’s back meets the wall, and Oliver braces a hand beside his shoulder.

**ETHAN:** “Morning.”

**OLIVER:** “About last night…”

Ethan glances at Oliver’s hand, then meets his eyes.

**ETHAN:** “I meant it.”

**ENDING POSE:** Oliver looks up at him, suddenly conscious of how close they are. Ethan’s smile fades into anticipation.

The elevator chimes.

---

#### Scene 9: The Second Confession

At the guild office, Vanessa closes a file.

**VANESSA:** “Oliver. There’s something you should know.”

**OLIVER:** “About the raid?”

She stands.

**VANESSA:** “I’m in love with you.”

Cut to Oliver’s shocked face.

**OLIVER:** “…You too?”

A slight zoom toward his eyes.

**ENDING POSE:** Freeze on Oliver, lips parted, still processing what he has just said.

**SOUND:** A bright, delicate short-drama **ding!**

Cut to black.

**Keep “You too?” as the final line.** It makes Oliver accidentally reveal the competing confession, giving Vanessa a reason to react—and giving you a strong cliffhanger without needing another scene.

For the pose endings, ask for a **brief natural hold** during generation: sustained eye contact, a hand remaining on a sleeve, or someone hesitating before speaking. Add the actual freeze-frame in editing if you want a more pronounced episode-ending effect.
