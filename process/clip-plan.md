**Generate these as separate sequences with cuts between shots.** Keep each romantic hold nearly still—eye contact, breathing, and subtle expression—so the pose reads clearly. For continuity, carry the same clothes through Clips 2–3, then use a distinct outfit for the next-morning elevator and final confession.
