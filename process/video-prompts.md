I generated the video as four 30-second clips with the **Seedance 2.5** model on **Higgsfield**. Each prompt went in with the [character sheets](characters-and-voices.md) attached as references. The dialogue, voices, music, and sound all came out of Seedance with the video. The prompts follow the [clip plan](clip-plan.md), rewritten as instructions for the video model: who each character is, what they sound like, what happens second by second, and what to leave out.

## Clip 1: Betrayal and reunion (0:00–0:30)

```prompt
Create a 30-second edited sequence for a modern urban fantasy romantic drama. Use the attached character references consistently. Premium cinematic semi-realism, realistic skin and clothing, subtle manhwa-inspired beauty, expressive acting, contemporary Boston-inspired setting. All romantic characters are adults aged 25 or older. Maintain the selected aspect ratio.
OLIVER: slender blond British man, blue eyes, navy hoodie over a white T-shirt, charcoal trousers. Light, warm adult voice with a natural contemporary southern English accent.
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: match his attached reference exactly. Masculine medium-low voice, mostly smooth with a subtle gravelly edge, natural American English, measured, warm, self-assured, quietly commanding.
CELESTE: glamorous adult brunette with immaculate styling, sharp brows and a polished, cold American voice. Her cruelty is controlled and deliberate.
0–7 seconds:
Apartment hallway. Celeste throws Oliver’s soft overnight bag through the open doorway. Clothes spill onto the floor. Her adult girlfriend stands behind her inside the apartment.
Oliver looks between them, hurt.
OLIVER: “You said she was just a friend.”
Celeste gives a small, dismissive smile.
CELESTE: “And you believed me?”
7–14 seconds:
Oliver kneels to gather his belongings.
OLIVER: “Where am I supposed to go?”
CELESTE: “That’s your problem.”
Briefly hold the composition: Oliver kneeling in the foreground, Celeste standing above him in the doorway. She closes the door. Sharp door slam.
14–22 seconds:
Cut to the pavement outside at dusk. Oliver struggles with his bag, trying to compose himself. His childhood friend approaches and recognizes him.
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: “Ollie?”
OLIVER: “Please don’t ask.”
His friend’s expression softens. He takes the bag.
22–30 seconds:
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: “Come home with me.”
OLIVER: “For tonight?”
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: “As long as you need.”
Close two-shot. Oliver looks up, surprised and relieved. His friend meets his eyes. Hold their final pose for one second with subtle breathing.
Use natural lip-synced dialogue, restrained emotional music, street ambience and clear speaker separation. No subtitles, captions, watermarks or additional dialogue.
```

## Clip 2: A new morning and a dangerous world (0:30–1:00)

```prompt
Create a 30-second edited continuation of a modern urban fantasy romantic drama. Match the attached character sheets exactly. Premium cinematic semi-realism, realistic people and environments, subtly idealized manhwa-inspired beauty. Contemporary Boston-inspired city. All romantic characters are adults aged 25 or older. Maintain the selected aspect ratio.
OLIVER: slender blond man with blue eyes, navy hoodie, white T-shirt, charcoal trousers. Light, warm contemporary southern English voice.
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: match reference. Masculine medium-low voice, subtle gravel, natural American English, warm and measured.
VANESSA: match reference, long chestnut hair, ivory blouse, fitted dark waistcoat, tailored trousers, restrained gold details and sword. Graceful, composed, respected guild leader.
MIO: match creature reference exactly, small enough to perch on Oliver’s shoulder. Blue-violet fur, white chest, floppy ears, one enormous plume tail. Youthful gender-ambiguous voice, high but pleasant, clear American English, bouncy, cheeky and self-important.
NARRATOR: separate adult voice, clear, composed, cinematic American English.
0–6 seconds:
Warm morning in the childhood friend’s modern apartment. Quick inserts of two coffee mugs and Oliver putting on his hoodie. His friend straightens the white T-shirt collar beneath it.
OLIVER, gently teasing: “You’re enjoying this.”
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: “You missed a spot.”
Hold briefly on their close eye contact before cutting away.
6–14 seconds:
They walk through a contemporary Boston-inspired city toward a glass office tower. Intercut a hunter generating magic and faint dark veins spreading beneath the hunter’s skin.
NARRATOR: “Cores give hunters power. But the gates they enter slowly poison them.”
14–20 seconds:
Show Oliver healing a seated adult hunter. Pale blue-gold light flows from his hands; the dark veins recede.
NARRATOR: “Oliver Ashford can undo that damage.”
20–25 seconds:
Brief flashback with a soft transition: Oliver kneels beside injured Mio and heals him. Return to the present, where Mio floats beside Oliver. A restrained translucent magical interface appears beside them.
MIO: “You saved me. Now you get my help!”
25–30 seconds:
Inside the office tower, the childhood friend enters the Hunter Management department while Oliver continues into the guild area. Vanessa waits with her sheathed sword.
VANESSA: “Ready, Oliver?”
OLIVER: “Ready.”
Hold briefly on Vanessa leading confidently forward with Oliver beside her.
Use clean editing, natural lip sync, soft morning ambience transitioning into restrained fantasy music. Narration plays over visual inserts. No additional dialogue, subtitles or watermarks. Keep the magical interface simple, without dense text.
```

## Clip 3: The raid and Vanessa's interest (1:00–1:30)

```prompt
Create a 30-second cinematic action-romance sequence for a modern urban fantasy drama. Match the attached Oliver and Vanessa references exactly. Preserve their faces, hair, proportions and outfits across every shot. Premium cinematic semi-realism, realistic skin and fabric, subtle manhwa-inspired beauty. All romantic characters are adults aged 25 or older. Maintain the selected aspect ratio.
OLIVER: slender blond adult man, blue eyes, navy hoodie over white T-shirt, charcoal trousers. Healer using pale blue-gold light. Light, warm contemporary southern English voice, slightly breathless when embarrassed.
VANESSA: athletic adult brunette, long half-swept-back chestnut hair, ivory blouse, dark fitted waistcoat, tailored trousers and boots, practical steel sword. Elegant, graceful, formidable and visibly in command. Medium-low American voice, lightly husky, clear and decisive; softer around Oliver.
0–6 seconds:
Inside a dangerous gate: ruined stone structures suspended in a dim supernatural landscape, thin violet toxic mist and drifting particles. Vanessa cuts down a shadow creature with one precise sword strike. Behind her, Oliver kneels beside a wounded teammate. His healing light clears dark corruption from the teammate’s arm.
VANESSA: “Stay behind me!”
6–12 seconds:
A second creature lunges toward Oliver from the side. Vanessa spots it.
VANESSA: “Oliver!”
She tackles Oliver clear of the attack. Show the action in readable stages: she reaches him, they fall together, then land. Her free hand protects the back of his head. Her sword points safely away from both bodies.
12–19 seconds:
Close two-shot. Oliver lies on his back; Vanessa braces above him, supporting her own weight.
VANESSA, concerned: “Are you hurt?”
OLIVER, flustered: “I… don’t think so.”
His hand lightly grips her sleeve. Their eyes lock and his cheeks flush. Hold this final pose for one second with subtle breathing and hair movement.
19–22 seconds:
Hard cut to the raid’s conclusion: the defeated creature dissolves, and the team exits as the gate closes behind them.
22–30 seconds:
Cut to Vanessa’s quiet guild office. Oliver stands close to her desk, healing a small cut on her hand.
OLIVER: “You risked yourself for me.”
VANESSA: “Of course I did.”
As Oliver starts to withdraw, she gently catches his hand. He looks up. Hold their eye contact and joined hands for the final second.
Use clear fight geography, natural body mechanics and expressive lip-synced acting. Action sound softens during eye contact, then transitions to quiet office ambience and restrained romantic music. No gore, additional dialogue, subtitles or watermarks.
```

## Clip 4: Two confessions (1:30–2:00)

```prompt
Create a 30-second edited romantic cliffhanger sequence for a modern urban fantasy drama. Match all attached character references exactly. Premium cinematic semi-realism, realistic skin, clothing and environments, subtly idealized manhwa-inspired beauty. All romantic characters are adults aged 25 or older. Maintain the selected aspect ratio.
OLIVER: slender blond British adult with blue eyes. Light, warm contemporary southern English voice, expressive and easily flustered.
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: match attached reference. Masculine medium-low voice, mostly smooth with subtle gravel, natural American English, measured, warm and quietly commanding. His confession reveals restrained vulnerability.
VANESSA: match attached reference. Graceful brunette guildmaster in ivory blouse and dark waistcoat. Medium-low, lightly husky American voice, direct and sincere.
0–5 seconds:
Evening, inside the childhood friend’s apartment entryway. Both have just arrived home. Oliver wears his navy hoodie and white T-shirt. His friend notices a little dried blood on Oliver’s collar.
CHILDHOOD FRIEND, concerned: “You’re hurt?”
OLIVER: “It isn’t mine.”
5–14 seconds:
His friend moves closer; Oliver takes a small step back toward the wall. Before Oliver’s head touches it, his friend places one palm behind his head to cushion it, with that forearm braced beside him. His other arm remains relaxed, leaving Oliver space to move.
CHILDHOOD FRIEND, quietly: “I’ve liked you since we were kids.”
OLIVER, surprised: “You… have?”
Oliver lightly grips the front of his friend’s coat. Hold their close eye contact for one second. His friend waits for a response.
14–23 seconds:
Clear cut to the next morning. Oliver now wears a pale blue sweater over a white shirt; his friend wears a fresh work outfit. In the apartment elevator, several adult commuters enter, gently crowding them together.
Reverse the previous composition: the friend’s back is against the elevator wall, and Oliver braces one hand beside his shoulder.
OLIVER, softly: “About last night…”
CHILDHOOD FRIEND: “I meant it.”
Hold their close pose and eye contact for one second. Elevator chime.
23–30 seconds:
Hard cut to Vanessa’s guild office. Oliver remains in his pale blue sweater. Vanessa stands, meets his eyes and speaks with calm sincerity.
VANESSA: “Oliver. I’m in love with you.”
Cut to Oliver’s startled face.
OLIVER: “…You too?”
Slow, slight push-in toward his face. Freeze briefly on his shocked expression. Play one bright, delicate short-drama “ding!” and cut to black.
Keep each confession intimate and emotionally believable. Use natural lip sync, clear speaker separation, restrained romantic music and brief silence before the final reaction. No kissing, additional dialogue, subtitles or watermarks.
```
