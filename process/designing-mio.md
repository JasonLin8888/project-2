Mio is the small magical creature Oliver saves, who gives him his system. These are my prompts to the image generator, in order, from the **mascot** tab of my [inspiration doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17gIm47t000_5_PasGklTHhs3f8hmvAtOcnp2sxa1ZIo/edit).

## The prompts, round by round

1. help me create a cute adorable mascot for a shortform drama I am creating. I was thinking of mixing a cat's personality with rabbit ears, a hyrax/wombat body, with a shrew's cuteness/smolness, a teddy bear's form, a squirell's cute and bushy tail. Potentially a panda's coloring, specifically red panda. Also I want to see it with and without a lion's mane
2. can you mess with the colors and try different fantasy pallet's like you would see in manhwa or anime. create one for each pallet/color. I also want more of a squirrel tail. currently it seems too much like a red panda. Also make the face more like a cat's.
3. I like the head markings you added. I especially like the celestial royal pallete but I want to see more variations just to make sure this is the best. I also want to see you mess with the ear shapes
4. I like the previous head markings more, because they were more subtle. I am in between top right and bottom left for the ears. I liked the body more in the previous one. Can you keep experimenting and also include different poses such as loafing (like a cat), L laying like a bunny does, situp on 4 paws like a cat, begging/standing on 2 hind legs, stretching/downward dog, and cleaning ears/face
5. I like the poses, but I think the colors are too dull now. Can you make the colors more vibrant and add markings
6. soften the head markings again. I want to experiment more with the style. Draw inspiration from league of legends, genshin impact, popular action or romance manhwa (especially the system trope), and 2010s animes. Also try to think of some more poses
7. make sure not to lose the cat/feline face
8. more ideas, also the bunny face is back, don't forget feline face. Here are some inspo pics
9. go back to the older pics
10. I want to go back to the older markings
11. don't forget the feline faces
12. I am trying to decide which mascot design I like most. Can you combine them to see if I like that version?

## The result

**Miozareth Aurelion, Keeper of the Azure Flame, Guardian of the Seventh Heaven.** Nickname: **Mio**.
