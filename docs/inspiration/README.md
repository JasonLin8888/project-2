# Inspiration

Additional sources of inspiration for my Unit 2 project, an **AI short drama**: a short, emotional fantasy story (working idea: a quiet, over-mature girl, a magic paintbrush, an ink creature, and a dragon on a scroll) made with generative video, voice, and music tools.

Copied 2026-10-07 from the two tabs of the Google Doc [*TDM155AI - Media Inspiration Board*](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17gIm47t000_5_PasGklTHhs3f8hmvAtOcnp2sxa1ZIo/edit?usp=sharing). The Google Doc is the original; if it changes, copy it here again.

- **[Media inspiration board](inspiration-board.md)** (tab 1): the AI dramas and fight scenes I want to learn from and what I like about each, then places to look for story, characters, creatures, and visual design; the Zhang Sengyou dragon legend and how to animate an ink dragon; and how to build the emotional core around a child character and a mascot animal.
- **[Preventing common errors](preventing-common-errors.md)** (tab 2): splitting the film into numbered shots and separate audio so fixes stay small, and keeping characters consistent with character sheets, locked descriptions, and reference images.

Most of the source lists and craft notes were written with AI. Treat them as leads to check: when you watch, read, or try something from here, put what you found in your [Notebook](/notebook), and add anything you still need to read to [Readings](../readings/README.md).
