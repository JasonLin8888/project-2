## The project I almost made: AI for dance production

> Completely new idea: using AI for dance production (creating choreo and teaching it).
>
> What if I have a vertical playing on the left and the words can come out to the left with my comments, and I can mark lyrics I want to embody in the choreo and cool things the instruments do.
>
> Secondary view is having it play to the right, like the website choreographers make their dancers leave comments on the video, and so it would have little comment boxes pop up out of the horizontal audio player. This also allows for a video above.
>
> Maybe make AI either automatically do the editing or something else as another feature of my end product. Could also be for two purposes: 1) teach choreo 2) edit the video for publicity purposes and posting on YouTube for audiences.
>
> Include the ticking back which shows the half counts, also helpful for teaching choreo and also remembering the counts when trying to create choreo to the music. Incorporate the comment feature/tags or some way to take notes on the counts. Would have to assimilate and flow smoothly with the other features so a choreographer could progress through the steps.
>
> Side idea that came to me: a translation feature like in Spotify for non-English songs, so it helps choreographers remember what the lyrics mean, and also helpful for dancers when they are learning choreo.
