## Side characters and comedy

- **Bella in *There's No Such Thing as a Bad Lady***: a great relief character: comedy for the audience and personality building for a main cast member. Also a great foil.
- **Chang in *Chronicles of a Doomed Prodigy***: a sidekick character that is cringey and fulfills a narrator role where he does the cringey introductions, but he's so comedic. He's there as a comedic relief, a sidekick character, a narrator for the audience, and adds personality to the story and cast of characters. A character the audience grows to love just as much as the actual mc.
- **[Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dem66MlR3bi/)**: Entertaining AI cartoon.
- **[Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfXO0XJPE7C/)**: Ts is funny af but I think it's a bit too corny. It's too niche. I do like the idea of exaggerating an issue you'd talk with friends about into some epic magical fight.
