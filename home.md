Thrown out of his apartment by the girlfriend who cheated on him, Oliver Ashford is taken in by his childhood friend. But Oliver isn't ordinary. In his city, hunters draw magic from a core that slowly corrupts them, and Oliver is a healer who can undo that corruption, with a system granted by a small magical creature he once saved. On a gate raid, his fierce guildmaster saves his life. That night, his childhood friend confesses he's loved him since they were kids. The next morning, his guildmaster confesses too.

I made this AI short drama to test the limits of current AI video generation. Along the way I learned more than how to prompt: I found reference photos, built character sheets, wrote a script around the tropes of the genre, and edited the clips into one story.
